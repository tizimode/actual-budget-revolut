Privacy Notice — Actual Budget Revolut

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Actual Budget Revolut is a private, self-hosted personal-use application operated solely by its owner.

The application uses Enable Banking to retrieve read-only payment-account information that the owner explicitly authorises through their bank. It does not initiate payments or transfers.

Transaction and account data are used only to import transactions into the owner’s self-hosted Actual Budget instance. The operator does not sell, share, rent, or disclose this data to third parties.

Data are retained only in the owner’s self-hosted Actual Budget installation, subject to the owner’s own backup and retention practices.

Access may be withdrawn at any time through Enable Banking or the relevant bank.
